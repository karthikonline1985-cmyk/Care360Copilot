The patient's July 5 ER visit was for a fall injury.

   RECOMMENDATION: Taper and discontinue gabapentin. Consider topical lidocaine patches or physical therapy referral for back pain as safer alternatives.

3. ELECTROLYTE MONITORING

4. LEVOTHYROXINE TIMING INTERACTION
   Levothyroxine absorption is reduced by omeprazole (gastric acid suppression) and calcium/iron supplements. If taken concurrently, thyroid levels may be subtherapeutic.

   RECOMMENDATION: Ensure levothyroxine is taken on an empty stomach, at least 60 minutes before omeprazole and any other medications. Recheck TSH in 6 weeks.
